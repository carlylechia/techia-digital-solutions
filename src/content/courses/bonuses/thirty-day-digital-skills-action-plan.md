# 30-Day Digital Skills Action Plan

By teChia Digital Academy
Digitalize. Simplify. Grow.

## Purpose

This 30-day plan helps full-pack buyers move from learning to action.

The goal is not to finish everything quickly. The goal is to build momentum, practice consistently, and create proof of your skills.

## How to Use This Plan

Each week has a focus:

Week 1: Foundation
Week 2: Visibility and Content
Week 3: Productivity and Tools
Week 4: Application and Proof

You can adjust the pace based on your schedule.
